Credits

Museum Manager was made in Unity in 2018 by ryseymour, sourseymour and Cole Wrampelmeier (colewrus). The design, rules, artist fact sheets, trivia, title art and interface art in this edition all come from that project's FinalRound branch.

This browser edition was rebuilt in 2026 from that branch.

The paintings are public-domain works by Vincent van Gogh, Leonardo da Vinci, Élisabeth Vigée Le Brun and Emperor Huizong, from the image files in the original project (via Wikimedia Commons).
